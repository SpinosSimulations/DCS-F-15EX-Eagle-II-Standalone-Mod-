F-15EX V3 Changelog

	A new version of the F-15EX. Not made or supported by Eagle Dynamics.

	Flight Model:
	- New external flight model of its own for all three variants (no longer borrows the F-15C flight model)
	- F110-GE-129 engines
	- Real internal fuel: 13,550 lb (F-15EX), 23,305 lb with CFTs (F-15EX (CFTs), F-15IA)
	- CFT variants have their own empty weight, CG, inertia and CFT drag
	- Fly-by-wire with g and AoA limiting: 30 deg AoA limit, 25 deg on the CFT variants
	- Reworked store drag
	- Reworked landing gear, brakes and nosewheel steering

	Avionics (all three variants):
	- New standalone cockpit avionics, replacing the FC3 F-15C systems
	- Large area display (LAD) touchscreen with selectable windows and formats
	- Engine / fuel display (shows the CFT tanks on the CFT variants)
	- AN/APG-82 AESA radar with track-while-scan and AIM-120 guidance
	- LPI radar: other aircraft's RWRs don't see the radar's search, in single player and multiplayer; a lock warns as normal
	- HUD and helmet-mounted display with head tracking; high off-boresight AIM-9X / Python-5
	- TEWS / RWR display, chaff and flares
	- Datalink picture from friendly AWACS, ground radars and fighters
	- Cold start, electrical system, INS alignment and navigation pages
	- Two ARC-210 V/UHF radios with mission editor presets
	- Voice warnings
	- The helmet display needs Scripts\Hooks\F15EX_HMD_hook.lua (see the README)

	Weapons:
	- AIM-120C-5, C-7, C-8, D, D-3, AIM-174B, AIM-260A and AIM-200A as separate weapons, all available at once
	- AIM-174B with its own model (thanks to currenthill)
	- Air-to-ground weapons removed for now (targeting pods, IRST pod and fuel tanks kept)

	Loadouts:
	- 15 single stations: wing rails and fuselage / CFT stations can be loaded one by one
	- Every missile on every station that can carry it
	- 26 mission editor presets for each variant

	Mod:
	- Mod folder renamed to F-15EX V3
	- Removed the unused Eagle Vision and Sensors folders
